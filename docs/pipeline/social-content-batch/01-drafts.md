## Source of truth for facts used

Pulled live from `app/page.tsx` and `lib/pipeline/dealRules.ts` in this repo:
- Tracks hotel prices daily across **20 destinations**.
- A deal is flagged only when price drops **30%+ below its 60-day rolling median**, and only
  after **at least 8 price checks (snapshots)** exist for that hotel (`DEAL_THRESHOLD = 0.70`,
  `MIN_SNAPSHOTS = 8` in `lib/pipeline/dealRules.ts`).
- Prices compared across **Expedia, Booking.com, Kiwi, Trip.com**.
- expaify never takes payment — user always books directly with the real marketplace.
- Free tier: **3 unlocked deals/week**, full feed browsable blurred, no email alerts.
- Premium: **$8/month billed annually (2 months free)**, unlimited unlocked deals, instant +
  daily email alerts, destination watchlist, filter by discount/stars/price, **7-day free trial**.

Copy was generated by Krater (`anthropic/claude-opus-5`, confirmed available and used for all
five platforms — no downgrade needed) with these facts plus each account's real current
bio/follower context (pulled live from Zernio `GET /accounts`) as grounding context, and an
explicit instruction not to invent stats beyond the facts above.
